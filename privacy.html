Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

At ManaRent HUB (accessible at https://manarent.onrender.com), your privacy is one of our top priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by ManaRent and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit or publish property/vehicle listings on ManaRent, we may collect the following information:

  • Listing Information: Titles, landmarks, subcity areas, prices, specifications, and owner names.
  • Contact Details: Phone numbers and WhatsApp numbers voluntarily submitted by property owners to connect with potential tenants.
  • Automated Log Data: IP addresses, browser type, date/time stamps, and page view statistics used for platform analytics and DDoS security defense.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, and display hyperlocal rental marketplace listings across cities.
  • To facilitate direct WhatsApp/call connections between tenants and verified property owners.
  • To analyze web traffic and prevent malicious bot scraping and automated security threats.
  • To serve relevant third-party advertisements via Google AdSense.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Cookies

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to ManaRent and other sites on the internet. Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. Data Protection & Security

ManaRent employs 256-bit SSL encryption and automated anti-bot security filters to protect your data against unauthorized access, disclosure, or modification.
